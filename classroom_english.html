🔍 科技與銲接關鍵字彙 (Vocabulary)

Semiconductor
半導體
Materials that conduct electricity under certain conditions.
Conductor
導體
Materials that allow electricity to flow easily (e.g., copper wire / 銅線).
Insulator
絕緣體
Materials that block the flow of electricity (e.g., plastic, rubber / 塑膠、橡膠).
Poor Conductor
不良導體
Materials that conduct electricity poorly, showing high resistance (e.g., pencil lead, tap water / 鉛筆芯、自來水).
Pencil Lead
鉛筆芯
A carbon-based material (graphite) that acts as a poor conductor.
LED (Light Emitting Diode)
發光二極體
A semiconductor light source that emits light when current flows.
Anode
正極
The long leg of the LED (+). Connects to positive voltage.
Cathode
負極
The short leg of the LED (-). Connects to Ground/Negative.
Polarity
極性
Possessing distinct positive and negative terminals.
Soldering Iron
電烙鐵
The hand tool used to heat and melt solder wire.
Solder Wire
銲錫絲
The fusible metal alloy used to join pins to the circuit board.
Safety Goggles
護目鏡
Protective eyewear designed to keep hot solder splashes away.
Multimeter
三用電表
The tool used to measure resistance, voltage, and current.
Computing / Processing
運算 / 處理
Processing data and instructions in CPUs and microcontrollers.
Switching / Control
開關 / 控制
Controlling electrical current flow using transistors.

💬 課堂情境口說引導 (Classroom Dialogues)

"Good morning, everyone! Welcome to our Life Technology class."
大家早安！歡迎來到我們的生活科技課。
Opening
"Last class, we used a multimeter to measure different materials. Do you remember what conductors, poor conductors, and insulators are?"
上節課我們使用三用電表測量了不同的材料。你們還記得什麼是導體、不良導體和絕緣體嗎？
Review
"Why did the multimeter show a high resistance value for pencil lead? Because pencil lead is a poor conductor!"
為什麼三用電表測量鉛筆芯時會顯示高電阻值？因為鉛筆芯是不良導體！
Review
"Today, we are going to explore the world of semiconductors and learn how to solder!"
今天我們要一起來探索半導體的世界，並學習如何銲接！
Opening
"Are semiconductors only used for sensing? No! They are also used for computing, switching, and emitting light!"
半導體只用於感測嗎？不！它們還可以用於運算、開關切換和發光！
Opening
"Rule 1: Don't play."
規則一：嚴禁嬉戲。
Safety Warning
"Rule 2: Don't burn anything."
規則二：不要燒焦任何物品。
Safety Warning
"Rule 3: Don't waste solder."
規則三：不要浪費銲錫。
Safety Warning
"Rule 4: Don't leave your group table."
規則四：不要離開小組座位。
Safety Warning
"Rule 5: Avoid touching metal."
規則五：避免碰觸金屬。
Safety Warning
"Let's practice the four steps of soldering: Heat the joint, Apply the solder, Remove the solder, Remove the iron."
我們來練習銲接的四個步驟：加熱接點、加銲錫、移開錫絲、移開烙鐵。
Instruction
"Step 1: Heat the joint with the iron for 2 seconds. Step 2: Apply the solder wire to melt it. Step 3: Remove the solder wire. Step 4: Remove the iron."
第一步：加熱接點 2 秒。第二步：靠上錫絲使其融化。第三步：移開錫絲。第四步：移開電烙鐵。
Instruction
"Look at your solder joint. Is it a volcano shape? It should be smooth and shiny."
看看你的銲點，是火山形狀嗎？它應該要光滑而且亮亮的。
Quality Check
"Now, connect your soldered LED to your pre-made Light-Sensing Circuit."
現在，把你焊好的 LED 連接到先前做好的光感應電路。
Testing
"Cover the LDR light sensor with your hand. Does the LED light up?"
用手遮住 LDR 光感應器。LED 有亮起來嗎？
Testing
"If it doesn't light up, check the polarity. Did you swap the positive and negative legs?"
如果不亮，檢查看看極性。你是不是把正負極腳接反了？
Troubleshooting
"Time is up! Please unplug your soldering irons."
時間到！請拔掉電烙鐵插頭。
Clean-up
"Let's review: What is a conductor? What is an insulator? And what is a semiconductor?"
我們來複習一下：什麼是導體？什麼是絕緣體？那半導體又是什麼？
Review
"A conductor allows electricity to flow, an insulator blocks it, and a semiconductor controls it under specific conditions."
導體讓電流通過，絕緣體阻擋電流，而半導體在特定條件下控制電流。
Review
"Clean your desk, and wash your hands with soap immediately."
整理桌面，並立刻去用肥皂洗手。
